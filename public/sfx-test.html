How to read this
Open this page as a browser source inside OBS to test what OBS can do, and in a normal browser to test that. The answers can differ.

Sound is working here means the files decoded and the audio output is live. If the overlay is still silent after that, its URL is missing ?sfx=1, or the source needs a right-click → Refresh.

Waiting for a click is normal in a browser and never happens in OBS: no page may make sound until you have clicked it once. Press any PLAY button and it clears.

found but could not be decoded means the file is not really the format its name claims. Re-export it as a plain MP3 or WAV.

If a PLAY button is silent while this page says everything is fine, the sound is not reaching your ears rather than not playing: in OBS tick Control audio via OBS in the source Properties and check the Audio Mixer, and in Windows check that the browser is not muted in the Volume Mixer.
