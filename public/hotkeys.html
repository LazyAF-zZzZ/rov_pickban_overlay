Control panel hotkeys
Click CHANGE, then press the key you want
These work on the Control Panel while its window has focus. A browser page cannot register system-wide shortcuts, so they will not fire while you are in OBS or the game.

Tapping a modifier on its own (Alt, Ctrl, Shift) is allowed and is the default for the banner toggle. It fires on release, and only when the key is tapped alone - so Alt+Tab still switches windows without cutting your banner off air.
